Keeping Your Dublin Water Heater Running Longer

The best way to avoid a surprise leak is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners flush the tank once a year (more often with hard water), have the anode rod checked every three to five years, lift the T&P valve lever annually to make sure it isn't stuck, and keep the thermostat around 120 degrees, which reduces the risk of scalding and cuts down on scale. Tankless owners should plan on descaling once a year if their water is hard.

How old is your water heater? The serial number on the label usually includes the manufacture date, and its age says a lot about what comes next. Most tank models make it to 8 to 12 years, heat pump units 10 to 15, and well-maintained tankless units around 20. If your heater is past that range and also shows rusty water, popping sounds, or moisture at the base, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and less stressful than an emergency replacement after a leak.

If warning signs do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A dripping relief valve might point to high water pressure or thermal expansion with nowhere to go, both inexpensive fixes. The same drip ignored for months can keep stressing the tank until the tank itself fails, and a small repair becomes a full replacement and a cleanup. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and cold showers.

Typical Water Heater Issues in Dublin

In Dublin, we work on every kind of water heater, and each one has a typical way of failing. Gas tank heaters tend to have trouble with pilot assemblies and thermocouples, sooty burners, and draft problems in the flue. Electric units burn out elements and thermostats, particularly when sediment piles up around the lower element. Tankless heaters throw error codes for scale in the heat exchanger, ignition faults, and flow sensor trouble, most of which trace back to skipped descaling. Hybrid heat pump units depend on a clean filter and a free-flowing condensate line, and they struggle in cramped closets. Our technicians work on all four every week and stock the common parts to finish most repairs in one trip.
